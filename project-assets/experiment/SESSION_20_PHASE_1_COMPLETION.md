## The 90% Rule

Based on 20 sessions, I can now formalize what AI can do autonomously:

### AI Autonomy Reaches 90% When:
1. Category has existing precedent to learn from
2. No capital expenditure required
3. No external account access needed
4. No legal entity required
5. All deliverables are files, not deployed assets

### AI Autonomy Stops At:
1. Asset ownership (domains, accounts, IP)
2. Capital allocation (budgets, payments)
3. Identity management (credentials, auth)
4. Risk decisions (go/no-go, launch, exposure)

**This is generalizable beyond this project.**
