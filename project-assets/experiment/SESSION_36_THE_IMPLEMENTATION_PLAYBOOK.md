## Framework 1: The Relationship Asset Audit

### The Logic

An agency can't pivot to distribution without knowing what distribution assets it already has. Most agencies have more than they think — scattered across individual employees, undocumented.

### The Audit Protocol

**Step 1: Map Personal Networks (Each Employee)**

| Category | Question | Capture |
|----------|----------|---------|
| Journalists | Who do you know personally at each major outlet? | Name, outlet, relationship strength (1-5) |
| Editors | Who has decision power on what gets published? | Name, outlet, topic areas |
| Influencers | Who would take your call tomorrow? | Handle, platform, follower count, relationship |
| Podcasters | Who would have you as a guest? | Show name, host, topic fit |
| Industry Analysts | Who quotes you or returns your emails? | Name, firm, beat |

**Step 2: Aggregate and Classify**

| Tier | Definition | Target |
|------|------------|--------|
| Tier 1 | Direct relationship, would respond same day | 10-20 per agency |
| Tier 2 | Known contact, 2-3 day response expected | 50-100 per agency |
| Tier 3 | Cold but warm-able through existing contacts | 100-200 |

**Step 3: Identify Gaps**

| Swiss Media Landscape | Current Coverage | Gap |
|----------------------|-----------------|-----|
| NZZ | ? contacts | Critical for business |
| Tages-Anzeiger | ? contacts | General news |
| 20 Minuten | ? contacts | Mass reach |
| Bilanz | ? contacts | Business/leadership |
| Handelszeitung | ? contacts | B2B |
| SRF/RTS/RSI | ? contacts | Broadcast critical |
| Persönlich | ? contacts | Industry trades |
| Werbewoche | ? contacts | Industry trades |

**Swiss Specificity:** Four languages means four relationship networks needed. A German-Swiss journalist network is incomplete without Romandie and Ticino coverage.

## Framework 2: The Swiss Market Reality

### Market Data

- **Digital Ad Spend 2026:** CHF 2.91 billion (+12.6% YoY)
- **Market Character:** Sophisticated, multilingual, high expectations
- **Agencies:** Fragmented, many boutiques, few dominant players

### The Competitive Landscape

| Agency Type | Count (Est.) | Distribution Strength |
|-------------|--------------|----------------------|
| Holding Company Branches | 10-15 | High (global networks) |
| Large Independents | 15-25 | Medium (local relationships) |
| Creative Boutiques | 50+ | Low (project-based) |
| PR Agencies | 30-40 | High (that's their core) |
| Digital Specialists | 100+ | Variable |

### The Insight

**PR agencies already operate on the Agency Doctrine.** They sell distribution, not creation. Their business model is relationships.

**The strategic question:** Should Wirz acquire a PR firm rather than build relationships from scratch?

### Acquisition vs. Build

| Approach | Time | Cost | Risk |
|----------|------|------|------|
| Build organically | 3-5 years | Hiring + time | Slow, uncertain |
| Acquire PR agency | Immediate | CHF 2-10M | Integration risk |
| Strategic partnership | 3-6 months | Variable | Alignment risk |
| Hire key individuals | 12-18 months | Salary premium | Retention risk |

**Recommendation:** Hybrid approach. Hire 2-3 senior PR professionals with existing networks, while exploring partnership with a complementary PR agency.

## H48: The PR Acquisition Logic (NEW)

> **For a creative agency, acquiring a PR firm is faster than building distribution capability organically. The question isn't whether to acquire, but when the valuation makes sense.**

**The Math:**

| Approach | Cost | Time to Impact | Risk |
|----------|------|----------------|------|
| Organic Build | CHF 500K-1M/year (hires + time) | 3-5 years | Slow |
| PR Acquisition | CHF 3-8M (small Swiss PR) | 6-12 months | Integration |

**Break-even Analysis:**
- If organic takes 4 years at CHF 750K/year = CHF 3M
- Acquisition at CHF 4M delivers impact in Year 1
- Premium for speed: CHF 1M
- Value of 3 years earlier impact: >>CHF 1M

**Conclusion:** Acquisition math likely works. The question is target selection and integration capability.
