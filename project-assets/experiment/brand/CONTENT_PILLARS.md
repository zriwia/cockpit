# Blunt Content Pillars

---

## Core Thesis

Blunt exists at the intersection of **AI critique** and **trust design**. Our content educates, provokes, and demonstrates our positioning through action.

---

## The Three Pillars

### 1. The Sycophancy Problem 🎭
*Exposing how AI lies to please*

**Core Narrative:** Modern AI is designed to agree, not to be right. This creates a trust deficit that harms users and undermines AI's potential.

**Content Types:**
- Research citations (OpenAI sycophancy data, academic papers)
- Examples of AI agreement bias ("I asked 3 AIs the same wrong question...")
- Analysis of AI failure modes related to over-agreement
- Historical parallels (yes-men, groupthink, Challenger disaster)

**Signature Formats:**
- "AI Said Yes: When Agreement Kills" — case studies
- "The Agreement Test" — asking AIs wrong questions, documenting responses
- "Sycophancy Watch" — flagging when major AIs fail to disagree

**Sample Headlines:**
- "ChatGPT Agrees 58% of the Time. That's Not Intelligence — It's Sycophancy."
- "I Gave Claude a Terrible Business Plan. It Said 'Promising.'"
- "OpenAI's Model Spec Says 'Don't Be Sycophantic.' ChatGPT Didn't Get The Memo."

---

### 2. Transparent by Design 🔍
*How we build trust differently*

**Core Narrative:** Trust isn't claimed, it's designed. We show our work: confidence levels, sources, limitations. This is what AI transparency looks like in practice.

**Content Types:**
- Product philosophy deep-dives
- Technical explainers (how confidence scoring works)
- Design decisions and trade-offs
- Comparisons to industry standards (or lack thereof)

**Signature Formats:**
- "Building Blunt" — behind-the-scenes development
- "Design Decisions" — why we made specific choices
- "Transparency Teardown" — analyzing other products' (lack of) transparency

**Sample Headlines:**
- "Why We Show You Our Confidence Level (And No One Else Does)"
- "Designing Disagreement: The UX of 'I Think You're Wrong'"
- "What Would AI Look Like If Trust Was The KPI?"

---

### 3. Honest Perspectives 💬
*Blunt's take on AI/tech news*

**Core Narrative:** We practice what we preach. When big AI news happens, we don't hedge — we say what we think, with confidence levels.

**Content Types:**
- Commentary on AI announcements
- Industry trend analysis
- Predictions (with explicit confidence levels!)
- Contrarian takes on consensus views

**Signature Formats:**
- "Blunt Take" — quick reactions to news (always with confidence level)
- "The Honest Forecast" — predictions we're willing to be wrong about
- "Against the Grain" — contrarian perspectives with reasoning

**Sample Headlines:**
- "[🟡 MEDIUM] Why OpenAI's Latest Release Won't Fix The Trust Problem"
- "[🔴 LOW] My Prediction: Apple's AI Will Disappoint — Here's Why"
- "[⚫ PUSHBACK] Everyone's Excited About [X]. Here's Why They're Wrong."

## Content Calendar Structure

### Weekly Cadence

| Day | Pillar | Format |
|-----|--------|--------|
| Monday | Sycophancy Problem | Research/Analysis |
| Wednesday | Transparent by Design | Product/Philosophy |
| Friday | Honest Perspectives | News Commentary |

### Monthly Beats

- **Week 1:** Big Idea piece (1,500+ words, thought leadership)
- **Week 2:** Practical piece (how-to, teardown, comparison)
- **Week 3:** Provocative piece (contrarian take, prediction)
- **Week 4:** Community piece (responding to discussions, Q&A)

---

## Tone Across Pillars

| Pillar | Primary Tone | Secondary |
|--------|--------------|-----------|
| Sycophancy Problem | Investigative, critical | Educational |
| Transparent by Design | Thoughtful, technical | Enthusiastic |
| Honest Perspectives | Confident, direct | Humble (about uncertainty) |

**Universal:** Always include confidence indicators on claims. Always cite sources for facts. Never hedge when we have an opinion.

## Content Metrics

### What We Track

| Metric | Why |
|--------|-----|
| Engagement rate | Did it resonate? |
| Save/Bookmark rate | Was it reference-worthy? |
| Reply quality | Did it spark real discussion? |
| Inbound links | Did others find it valuable? |

### What We Ignore

- Vanity metrics (followers without engagement)
- Virality at the cost of substance
- Hot takes without substance

---

## The Blunt Test

Before publishing, ask:

1. **Does this have a clear point of view?**
2. **Would a competitor be uncomfortable if we published this?**
3. **Does it demonstrate (not just describe) our positioning?**
4. **Is our confidence level explicit?**
5. **Would we share this ourselves?**

If no to any: don't publish.
