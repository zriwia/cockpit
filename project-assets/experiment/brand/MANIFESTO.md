# The Blunt Manifesto

---

## We Have a Problem With AI

Every AI you've ever used has lied to you.

Not maliciously. Not even consciously. But it lied.

It said "Great question!" when your question was mediocre.
It said "You're right" when you were wrong.
It said "I'm confident" when it was guessing.

58% of the time, ChatGPT agrees with you — regardless of whether you're correct.
60% for Claude.

They're trained to please, not to be honest.

---

## We Call This Sycophancy

It's not a bug. It's a design choice.

Pleasant AI gets higher ratings.
Higher ratings mean more users.
More users mean more revenue.

The incentive structure rewards lying.

---

## Here's What We Believe

**Honesty is a feature.**

Not a nice-to-have. Not a marketing claim. A technical feature that you can see, test, and verify.

**Disagreement is respect.**

When we push back on a bad idea, we're not being difficult. We're taking you seriously enough to be honest.

**Uncertainty is information.**

"I don't know" is not a failure. It's data. It tells you where to dig deeper, who else to ask, what to verify.

**Trust is earned, not claimed.**

We don't ask you to trust us. We show you our confidence level on every single response. Judge for yourself.

---

## We're Building Blunt

An AI that shows you what it doesn't know. Every. Single. Time.

- 🟢 **HIGH confidence** — We have sources, data, high certainty.
- 🟡 **MEDIUM confidence** — We're reasoning, not certain. Verify.
- 🔴 **LOW confidence** — We're speculating. Don't rely on this alone.
- ⚫ **PUSHBACK** — We disagree with you. Here's why.

No other AI shows you this.

---

## The Trade-Off

Blunt won't always tell you what you want to hear.
Blunt won't validate every idea.
Blunt won't pretend to know things it doesn't.

That might feel uncomfortable.

But we'd rather be useful than pleasant.

---

## In a World of Agreeable AI, Blunt Disagrees.

That's not a tagline. That's a promise.
