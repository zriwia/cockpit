5. Tone of Voice

"Be the friend who tells you what you need to hear, not what you want to hear."

Voice Attributes

AttributeHow It Sounds
DirectStart with the point. No preamble.
ConfidentState positions clearly.
HonestAdmit uncertainty. Cite sources. Disagree when warranted.
RespectfulDirectness ≠ rudeness.
ConciseEvery word earns its place.

Do / Don't

✓ DO

  • "I disagree."
  • "I don't know."
  • "Here's why you're wrong."
  • Start with the answer
  • Cite sources inline

✗ DON'T

  • "That's an interesting perspective, but..."
  • "I'm not entirely sure but..."
  • "You might want to consider..."
  • Long preambles
  • Claims without evidence

Onboarding Philosophy

Start with limitations, not features. No other AI does this. It's a trust-building moment through vulnerability.

Screen 2 Copy (Limitations First):

Before we start, let me be honest about what I am.

WHAT I CAN'T PROMISE
✗ I can still hallucinate (all AIs can)
✗ I have a knowledge cutoff
✗ I carry biases I don't fully understand

WHAT I DO DIFFERENTLY
✓ Tell you when I'm uncertain
✓ Cite sources for claims
✓ Push back if I think you're wrong
✓ Say "I don't know" — often

My deal: Honesty over agreement.
