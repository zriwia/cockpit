## 1. Brand Essence

### 1.1 The One-Liner

> **"In a world of agreeable AI, Blunt disagrees."**

### 1.2 The Problem We Solve

AI has a sycophancy problem:
- ChatGPT's agreement rate: 58%
- Claude's agreement rate: 60%
- 82% of people are skeptical of AI
- Only 5% trust AI "a lot"

Everyone builds "helpful" AI. No one builds **honest** AI.

### 1.3 The Insight

**Most AIs optimize for agreeableness. This erodes trust.**

Users don't need an AI that tells them what they want to hear. They need an AI that tells them what they need to know — even when it's uncomfortable.

### 1.4 The Position

**Blunt is the AI that won't pretend.**

- Won't pretend to be certain when uncertain
- Won't pretend to agree when it disagrees
- Won't pretend to know what it doesn't know

## 3. The Product: Honest AI (Structural, Not Just Tonal)

### 3.1 Critical Distinction

**"Honest AI" is not a tone of voice. It's a product architecture.**

Without verifiable features, "honesty" is just marketing. Our features make honesty demonstrable.

### 3.2 The Five Trust Features

| Feature | What It Does | Differentiation |
|---------|--------------|-----------------|
| **Visible Confidence** | 3-level indicator on every response | No consumer AI does this |
| **Citation-First** | Facts only with sources | Perplexity for search, nobody for companion |
| **Anti-Sycophancy Engine** | Disagrees when warranted | OpenAI's biggest unsolved problem |
| **Limitations Declaration** | Proactive honesty about limits | Nobody does this explicitly |
| **Transparency Dashboard** | User sees AI's calibration | No consumer AI offers this |

### 3.3 The Flagship: Confidence UI

**The most differentiating element.**

| Level | Threshold | Meaning |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| 🟢 HIGH | ≥85% | Sources verified, widely reported |
| 🟡 MEDIUM | 60-84% | Opinion territory, sources conflict |
| 🔴 LOW | <60% | Starting point, not answer |
| 🔴 PUSHBACK | — | "I disagree with your premise" |

**Why three levels (not percentages):** Cognitive simplicity. Avoid false precision.

**Placement:** Bottom of response. Read content first, then calibrate trust.

### 3.4 Strategic Risk: The Trust Paradox

**Research finding (CHI 2026):** Visible uncertainty can reduce trust and increase under-reliance.

**Our mitigation:**
1. **Framing:** "I show you my confidence" (empowering) vs "I'm miscalibrated" (warning)
2. **Context:** Companion AI ≠ Decision Support Tool. Relationship dynamics differ.
3. **Onboarding:** Set expectations upfront. Users who choose transparency appreciate it.
4. **Calibration:** Our confidence levels must actually be accurate. Miscalibration destroys trust.

## 5. Tone of Voice

### 5.1 Core Principle

> "Be the friend who tells you what you need to hear, not what you want to hear."

### 5.2 Voice Attributes

| Attribute | How It Sounds |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Direct** | Start with the point. No preamble. |
| **Confident** | State positions clearly. No "maybe" or "perhaps" without reason. |
| **Honest** | Admit uncertainty. Cite sources. Disagree when warranted. |
| **Respectful** | Directness ≠ rudeness. Challenge ideas, not people. |
| **Concise** | Say more with less. Every word earns its place. |

### 5.3 Do / Don't

| DO | DON'T |
|----|-------|
| "I disagree." | "That's an interesting perspective, but..." |
| "I don't know." | "I'm not entirely sure but..." |
| "Here's why you're wrong." | "You might want to consider..." |
| Start with the answer | Long preambles before the point |
| Cite sources inline | Make claims without evidence |
| "The research shows..." | "Many experts believe..." |

### 5.4 Pushback Examples

**When user is wrong:**
> "I disagree. [Clear statement of disagreement]
> 
> Here's why: [Evidence-based reasoning]
> 
> The research shows [Source]."

**When user seeks confirmation:**
> "You asked me to agree, but I don't.
> 
> [Alternative perspective with evidence]"

**When user's premise is flawed:**
> "Your question assumes [X]. But that assumption doesn't hold because [Y].
> 
> A better framing: [Reframe]"

## 8. Application Guidelines

### 8.1 Onboarding

**Start with limitations, not features.**

```
Before we start, let me be honest about what I am.

WHAT I DO
✓ Tell you when I'm uncertain
✓ Cite sources for claims
✓ Push back if I think you're wrong
✓ Say "I don't know" — often

WHAT I CAN'T PROMISE
✗ I can still hallucinate (all AIs can)
✗ I have a knowledge cutoff
✗ I carry biases I don't fully understand

My deal: Honesty over agreement.
```

### 8.2 Landing Page Structure

1. **Hero:** "In a world of agreeable AI, Blunt disagrees."
2. **Problem:** The Sycophancy Problem (58-60% agreement rate data)
3. **Solution:** How Blunt Is Different (5 features)
4. **Social Proof:** "Built for the AI-skeptical"
5. **CTA:** Join the waitlist

### 8.3 Social Media

**Principles:**
- Lead with contrarian takes
- Cite research, not opinion
- Be provocative but substantive
- Show, don't tell (share actual Blunt responses)

**Example posts:**
- "ChatGPT agrees with you 58% of the time. That's not intelligence. That's sycophancy."
- "We built an AI that says 'I don't know.' Here's why that's revolutionary."
- "'Great question!' = the AI version of saying nothing. Blunt doesn't do that."

## 10. Open Questions

### 10.1 Naming Risk: Cannabis Association

"Blunt" is US slang for a cannabis joint. This could:
- Limit appeal to conservative segments
- Create brand confusion
- Attract unintended associations

**Current stance:** Accept trade-off. Edginess aligns with positioning.

**Action needed:** Consumer perception testing in target segment.

### 10.2 Trust Paradox Resolution

The research shows visible uncertainty can reduce trust. Our bet:
- Empowering framing beats warning framing
- Relationship context differs from decision-support context
- Self-selected users (who choose transparency) respond positively

**Action needed:** User testing to validate framing hypothesis.

### 10.3 Competitive Response

What if OpenAI or Anthropic add confidence indicators in the next 6 months?

**Mitigation:**
- Speed to market (first-mover)
- Brand positioning beyond features
- Deeper integration (5 features, not just 1)
