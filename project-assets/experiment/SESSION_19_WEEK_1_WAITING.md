## The CSO-Level Insight

### From "Waiting" to "Governance Boundaries"

The waiting state has revealed something fundamental:

> **KI autonomy ends not at competence boundaries, but at governance boundaries.**

The blockers are not "tasks AI can't do." They are "decisions humans must make."

| Blocker | Category | Why Human-Required |
|---------|----------|-------------------|
| Domain | Asset Ownership | Legal entity must register |
| Budget | Capital Allocation | Whose money, what priority? |
| Backend Config | Account Access | Credentials are personal |
| Launch | Risk Decision | Accountability if it fails |

**Pattern Recognition:**
- AI can research → Human must decide
- AI can propose → Human must commit  
- AI can prepare → Human must own
- AI can execute → Human must authorize

### The Governance Stack

```
GOVERNANCE LAYER (Human Required)
├── Legal Ownership (Domain, IP, Contracts)
├── Capital Allocation (Budget, Spend Authority)
├── Identity (Accounts, Credentials, Auth)
└── Accountability (Launch, Go/No-Go, Risk)

EXECUTION LAYER (AI Capable)
├── Research & Analysis
├── Strategy & Positioning  
├── Creative Development
├── Technical Implementation
└── Documentation & Process
```

**Implication:** Tools that speed execution without addressing governance deliver limited value in organizational contexts.
