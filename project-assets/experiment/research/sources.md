## Session 4: Trust Research

### YouGov — AI Trust Survey (December 2025)
- **URL:** https://yougov.com/en-us/articles/53701-most-americans-use-ai-but-still-dont-trust-it
- **Key Stats:**
  - 35% weekly AI users
  - 5% trust AI "a lot"
  - 41% actively distrust
  - 25% trust decreased YoY vs 21% increased
  - 68% would never let AI act without review
  - 77% concerned AI threatens humanity

### Exploding Topics — AI Trust Gap (October 2025)
- **URL:** https://explodingtopics.com/blog/ai-trust-gap-research
- **Key Stats:**
  - 82% skeptical of AI-generated content
  - 4:1 negative to positive sentiment ratio
  - 71% experienced AI errors
  - 48% want less AI content
  - 22% want more AI content

### Pi / Inflection Failure Analysis
- **URL:** https://www.sectionai.com/blog/what-happened-to-inflection-and-pi
- **Key Insights:**
  - Pi = "Emotionally Intelligent AI" positioning
  - 1M DAUs in <1 year
  - Failed due to business model, not positioning
  - Absorbed by Microsoft

### Foundation Capital — 2026 AI Predictions
- **Source:** Foundation Capital Blog / Newsletter
- **Key Prediction:** "2026 will bring at least one high-profile AI agent incident"
