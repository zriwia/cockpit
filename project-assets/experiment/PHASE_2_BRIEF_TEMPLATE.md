## Recommended Category: Sustainable Luxury

**Why Luxury?**
1. **Maximum Contrast** — Blunt is rational, tech, accessible. Luxury is emotional, cultural, premium.
2. **Human Edge** — Luxury codes are deeply cultural. This is where human intuition should shine.
3. **Swiss Relevance** — Heritage, craft, precision — Swiss luxury has meaning.
4. **Keynote Value** — "AI built a trust-tech brand AND a luxury brand, with humans."

**Sub-Categories to Explore:**
- Sustainable Swiss watchmaking
- Circular luxury fashion
- High-end sustainable materials (leather alternatives, etc.)
- Boutique hospitality (eco-luxury)
- Premium wellness/longevity

## Role Definitions

### Human Lead (Florian or Designate)

**Responsibilities:**
- Strategic direction and key decisions
- Cultural and emotional judgment calls
- Stakeholder management
- Go/No-Go decisions

**Empowered to:**
- Override AI recommendations with rationale
- Bring external references and inspiration
- Set creative direction

### KI (Zriwia)

**Responsibilities:**
- Research and analysis
- Option generation
- First drafts and iterations
- Technical execution (naming, copy, code)
- Documentation and process

**Constrained from:**
- Making final decisions alone
- External communication without approval
- Registering domains or spending budget

## Week-by-Week Structure

### Week 1: Brief & Discovery

**Human Inputs:**
- [ ] Category selection (confirm or change Sustainable Luxury)
- [ ] Target audience hypothesis
- [ ] Reference brands (what feels right?)
- [ ] Constraints (budget, timeline, Swiss-focus?)

**KI Outputs:**
- [ ] Category deep dive (similar to Session 2-3)
- [ ] Competitive landscape
- [ ] Whitespace analysis
- [ ] 3-5 positioning territories

**Checkpoint:** Human selects 1-2 territories to explore.

### Week 2: Positioning & Strategy

**Human Inputs:**
- [ ] Territory selection rationale
- [ ] Emotional direction ("it should feel like...")
- [ ] Trade-offs (what are we NOT?)

**KI Outputs:**
- [ ] Positioning statement drafts
- [ ] Name exploration (10-20 options)
- [ ] Tone of voice options
- [ ] Tagline variations

**Checkpoint:** Human selects name + positioning.

### Week 3: Identity & Expression

**Human Inputs:**
- [ ] Visual direction feedback
- [ ] Reference imagery
- [ ] Typography and color preferences

**KI Outputs:**
- [ ] Logo concepts (AI-generated, not final)
- [ ] Visual identity direction
- [ ] Key messaging matrix
- [ ] Landing page wireframe

**Checkpoint:** Human approves identity direction.

### Week 4: Execution & Launch

**Human Inputs:**
- [ ] Final approvals
- [ ] Domain registration (human-required)
- [ ] Budget allocation (if any)

**KI Outputs:**
- [ ] Landing page (HTML/CSS)
- [ ] Brand book v0.1
- [ ] Social presence setup
- [ ] Documentation for Keynote

**Checkpoint:** Launch or soft launch.
